About

PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Bristol, specialising in efficient AI for image and video. Research spans neural image/video/3DGS compression (end-to-end codecs, implicit neural representations, scalable coding), model compression and acceleration (quantization, pruning, knowledge distillation and data-efficient training), generative modelling (flow matching, generative compression), and real-time video models.
