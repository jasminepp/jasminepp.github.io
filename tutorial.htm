Useful links and emails

Practical pointers for students at the University of Bristol.

Enquiries

For student letters, academic marks, student regulations, exams and resit exams:

  • School of Computer Science: coms-student-enquiries@bristol.ac.uk and coms-education@bristol.ac.uk
  • School of Electrical, Electronic and Mechanical Engineering: eeme-student-enquiries@bristol.ac.uk

Coursework extensions

Guidance on requesting a coursework extension is at bristol.ac.uk/students/support/academic-advice/assessment-support/request-a-coursework-extension.

Requests must be submitted 48 hours before the coursework deadline, and you should keep working to your current due date until the school approves the extension.

Exceptional circumstances

If the extension deadline has passed, or something unexpected, unavoidable and outside your control has happened, you can submit an exceptional circumstances case: bristol.ac.uk/students/support/academic-advice/assessment-support/exceptional-circumstances.

Key systems

  • Blackboard — ole.bris.ac.uk: teaching material, assessment details and submission portals (instructions).
  • eVision — evision.apps.bristol.ac.uk: assessment details and results (instructions).
